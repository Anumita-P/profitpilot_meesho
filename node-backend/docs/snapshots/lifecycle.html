Trigger hygiene

Every move passes these rules. Why each one exists:
Max step
8% per moveBuyers notice jumps above ~10%; a small step keeps the elasticity estimate local (we only trust β near the price we observed).
Min data
1,000 views = sanity checkAt 4% CTR × 12% CVR, 1,000 views ≈ 5 orders: enough to see buyers are ordering, far too few to measure price sensitivity. That is learned across similar products (pooled).
Cooldown
7 days per SKUOne full weekday + weekend cycle, so day-of-week swings are not mistaken for a price effect.
Stability
≤ 2 price moves / monthNo flapping: buyers and resellers who share links need to trust the price.
Floor
never below FEvery published price covers return-adjusted cost per kept order. Recovery floor only at Exit, with explicit consent.
Auto-revert
judge day 14 · confirm day 28Orders react within days, but returns arrive late (return-window lag). Day 14: if profit per impression is worse on orders, the price goes back. Day 28: confirmed on kept orders, once returns have matured.
Stage classifier · g = kept units, last 4 weeks ÷ the 4 weeks before − 1 (seasonal products: vs the same weeks last season), DOI = stock ÷ avg daily units.
Launch: age < 30 d · Growth: g > +15% · Maturity: |g| ≤ 15% · Decline: g < −15% or DOI > 60 · Exit: stock age > 90 d / season end.
e.g. romper g = 403 ÷ 328 kept units − 1 = +23% → Growth; vase DOI = 400 ÷ 5.5 = 73 days → Decline.
