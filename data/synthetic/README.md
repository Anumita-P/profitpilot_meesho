# Synthetic data

Generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` with seed 20260928 from `backend/app/ml/world.py`
(the ground-truth world; never called by seller-facing endpoints).

- `skus.csv` — 599 SKUs (9 hand-authored demo SKUs)
- `sku_daily_obs.csv` — 35940 SKU-days (M1 demand, M2 payment-mix training table)
- `order_events.csv` — 106816 order-level rows (M3 RTO, M4 return training table)

**Hidden confounder:** a category-level weekly demand shock drives both conversion and the
seller's own price rule on `price_source=seller_rule` days, so a naive regression of orders on
price is biased. `price_source=randomised_ladder` days draw the price independently of the
shock; they are the synthetic stand-in for the randomised price-experiment phase (roadmap) and
are what identify the causal price effect.

Customer identifiers are random UUIDs. No Meesho data, no PII.
